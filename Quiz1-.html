The Lost Notebook

A Walk After School

One afternoon, Daniel was walking home from school when he noticed a small notebook lying beside the road. He picked it up because he thought someone might have lost it.

The Mystery

Daniel opened the notebook and saw a name written on the first page. The name was Samuel. He also found a small drawing of a house near the river. Daniel became curious and decided to find the owner.
He carefully kept the notebook in his school bag.

Finding the Owner

The next morning, Daniel asked his teacher about the notebook. His teacher remembered a student named Samuel who lived near the river. Daniel was happy that he could return it. He wrote a small note and left it with the teacher.

Short Quotation

Daniel said, Doing the right thing feels good.

Block Quotation

A small act of honesty can make a big difference. Returning something that does not belong to you shows respect for other people.

What Daniel Did

  1. Daniel found the notebook.
  2. He looked inside the notebook.
  3. He asked his teacher for help.
  4. He returned the notebook to Samuel.

Things in the Story

  • A notebook
  • A school bag
  • A drawing of a house
  • A note

Important Words

Notebook
A book used for writing notes.
Teacher
A person who helps students learn.
Honesty
The quality of being truthful and fair.
